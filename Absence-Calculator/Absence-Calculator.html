Absence limit tracker

Semester 1, IT1a, 17 Aug to 12 Feb. Missing more than 20% of a subject's lessons gives a 1.0 in that subject. Excused absences count too, and so does arriving late.

What if I miss a whole day?

Whole days you can still miss

The tightest subject of that day decides. ÜK 187 has its own rules and is left out here.

How it counts: lessons you can still miss = 20% of the semester total, rounded down, minus what you already missed. The first lesson after that gives the 1.0.

Check these: lesson totals come from the timetable (ABU 84, Modul 117 66). If the school counts differently, for example ABU at 60, change the Total box. All absences start at 0, so enter the numbers from your Absenzenblatt.
