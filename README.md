# Personal-Experiments

A random collection of small experiments, half-finished ideas and things I built just to try stuff out. Some of it started as a quick test and got a bit bigger than planned. Some of it never got past the first attempt. Nothing in here is polished, and a lot of it is probably broken, outdated or missing pieces.

## What this is

This repo is a place to keep my own side experiments in one spot instead of leaving them scattered across my computer. It's mostly for learning, testing ideas and seeing what happens when I change things. Don't expect clean code, good structure or proper documentation.

## What this isn't

- Not a finished product or a portfolio of polished work
- Not maintained on any schedule
- Not guaranteed to run on your machine

## Notes

This is a work in progress. Things get added, changed, renamed or abandoned without warning. Some folders may be empty, half done or out of date. If you use anything from here, check it first and use it at your own risk.
